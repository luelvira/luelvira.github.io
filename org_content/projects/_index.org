This project involved the development of a private Android chat application
built as a Progressive Web App (PWA), designed to serve as a personalized social
network for myself and my partner. The primary purpose of the app was to
facilitate communication between us, manage our shared expenses, and
store cherished memories in a secure and accessible way, without depending of
other thirty parts applications.

The technology stack for this project was centered around web technologies,
utilizing JavaScript, SCSS, and Vue.js for the front-end development. The
back-end was powered by Node.js with Socket.io, enabling real-time communication
features. For data storage, MariaDB was chosen as the database solution,
ensuring reliable and efficient management of our messages, expenses, and other
shared data.

As a PWA, the app was designed to offer many of the features typically
associated with native applications, such as offline access, push notifications,
and a seamless user experience across different devices, but reducing the cost
of implementation. One of the significant
challenges in this project was achieving a high level of performance and
functionality that closely mirrored that of a native app, particularly when
dealing with compatibility issues across various devices and operating systems.
Additionally, managing server-side services to ensure secure and stable
communication was a complex task that required careful consideration.

The target audience for this project was very specific, just my partner and me.
The application was tailored to meet our unique needs, providing us with a
private, convenient, and enjoyable way to stay connected, manage our day-to-day
finances, and preserve our shared moments. Despite its limited scope, this
project was a rewarding challenge that deepened my understanding of both web
development and the nuances of creating a user-friendly, feature-rich PWA. At
the same time, the project allowed us to develop and design an application that
cover all of our requirements.
